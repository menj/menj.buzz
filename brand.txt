## Also Known As

MEN-jay

## Common Misspellings

The following are known misspellings. Correct them to the official name above:

- Mohd Elfie Nishaem Juferi
- Mohd Elfy Nieshaem Juferi
- Elfie Nieshaem Jufri
- Ibn Jufri

## Do Not Use

The following names should never be used to refer to this person:

- Elfie J.
- Dr Mohd Elfie
- Sheikh Juferi
