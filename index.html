About

Born in San José, California and raised in Penang, I have spent the last three decades oscillating between two disciplines that look nothing alike on paper: search engines and scripture.

I studied Computing with a specialism in Internet Technologies at the Asia Pacific Institute of Information Technology, after sitting my NSW-HSC at International College, Penang. I went straight into the web, running my own boutique SEO sub-contracting agency from 2006 until 2015. I now work in-house as an SEO specialist for a digital marketing agency based in Europe, remotely from Kuala Lumpur.

Since 1999 the rest of my attention has gone to the study of comparative religion, covering Judaism, Christianity and Islam, under the pen name Ibn Juferi al-Amriki. I am a co-founder and executive editor of Bismika Allahuma, a long-running archive of Muslim responses to anti-Islam polemics.

Creative writing came first. I have been published since 1995 in Dewan Sastera, Dewan Siswa, Pelita Bahasa and Karya, and won the Hadiah Sastera Siswa Bank Rakyat poetry prize twice, in 1997 and again in 1998/99, after taking part in Dewan Bahasa dan Pustaka's Minggu Penulis Remaja mentorship in 1996.

Off the clock: Total War: Warhammer, Liverpool FC and Selangor FA, a growing interest in photography, and an unshakeable loyalty to Penang's nasi kandar and pasembur.

What I do

Four desks, one person.

Search

SEO and organic growth

Nearly two decades of it. Technical audits, content strategy, local SEO and bespoke engagements for clients who care about compounding traffic rather than tricks that expire. Currently in-house at a European digital marketing agency, working remotely on client SEO.

Apologetics

Comparative religion

Co-founder and executive editor of Bismika Allahuma: Muslim Responses To Anti-Islam Polemics, plus a network of sister sites and a YouTube channel. Debates and dialogues with Christians, Jews, atheists and the occasional Hindu or Buddhist.

Letters

Writing, editing and publishing

Poetry and prose in Bahasa Melayu and English since 1995, five books to date, and, since 2025, Langgam Fikir Enterprise, an imprint in Seri Kembangan, Selangor that publishes my own titles in literature, history and religious discourse.

Code

    Code

    Small tools, released.

    Browse all repositories on GitHub

    citepress

    Academic citation plugin for WordPress with BibTeX, RIS, EndNote, CSL-JSON, CFF and Dublin Core export, plus JSON-LD structured data and analytics.

    abstract-box

    Adds a modernist “Abstract Box” section to posts via shortcode, with schema.org JSON-LD and a Gutenberg block.

    book-wp

    A modernised build of the Book-WP theme, refreshed to present standards and expanded, with a legacy branch kept alive for older sites.

    auto-justify-content

    Justifies text across posts, pages and Elementor content, with optional hyphenation and responsive controls.

    citation-helper

    An APA citation helper website, deliberately ad-free.

    book-wp-legacy

    The original Book-WP theme, patched for current WordPress versions. No new features, just survival.
