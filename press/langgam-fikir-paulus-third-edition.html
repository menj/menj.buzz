Langgam Fikir Enterprise announces the third edition of Paulus: Perosak Risalah al-Masīḥ

The Malay-language study of Pauline theology returns with a revised grammar audit and an expanded apparatus of sources.

Kuala Lumpur — 1 Sep 2026 — Langgam Fikir Enterprise today announced a third edition of Paulus: Perosak Risalah al-Masīḥ, the Malay-language study of Pauline theology first issued in 2025. The new edition carries a full grammar audit against the third edition of Tatabahasa Dewan, together with an expanded apparatus of primary sources.

The revision responds to reader correspondence received since the second printing, and standardises transliteration of Arabic terms throughout. An English companion volume remains in development.

The third edition will be distributed through the imprint's usual channels. Review copies are available to academic and religious-studies press on request.

“The argument has not changed. What has changed is the evidence a reader can check without leaving the page.”

Mohd Elfie Nieshaem Juferi, publisher, Langgam Fikir Enterprise

About

Langgam Fikir Enterprise is an independent imprint based in Seri Kembangan, Selangor, publishing in literature, history, ideas and religious discourse.
