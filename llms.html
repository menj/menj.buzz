Summary

A writer, apologist, SEO specialist and developer, working on scholarly resources for Muslim apologetics and building tools that keep the web open and honest.

I studied Computing with a specialism in Internet Technologies at the Asia Pacific Institute of Information Technology, after sitting my NSW-HSC at International College, Penang. I went straight into the web, running my own boutique SEO sub-contracting agency from 2006 until 2015. I now work in-house as an SEO specialist for a digital marketing agency based in Europe, remotely from Kuala Lumpur.

Since 1999 the rest of my attention has gone to the study of comparative religion, covering Judaism, Christianity and Islam, under the pen name Ibn Juferi al-Amriki. I am a co-founder and executive editor of Bismika Allahuma, a long-running archive of Muslim responses to anti-Islam polemics.

Creative writing came first. I have been published since 1995 in Dewan Sastera, Dewan Siswa, Pelita Bahasa and Karya, and won the Hadiah Sastera Siswa Bank Rakyat poetry prize twice, in 1997 and again in 1998/99, after taking part in Dewan Bahasa dan Pustaka's Minggu Penulis Remaja mentorship in 1996.

Off the clock: Total War: Warhammer, Liverpool FC and Selangor FA, a growing interest in photography, and an unshakeable loyalty to Penang's nasi kandar and pasembur.

What he does

  • SEO and organic growth — Nearly two decades of it. Technical audits, content strategy, local SEO and bespoke engagements for clients who care about compounding traffic rather than tricks that expire. Currently in-house at a European digital marketing agency, working remotely on client SEO.
  • Comparative religion — Co-founder and executive editor of Bismika Allahuma: Muslim Responses To Anti-Islam Polemics, plus a network of sister sites and a YouTube channel. Debates and dialogues with Christians, Jews, atheists and the occasional Hindu or Buddhist.
  • Writing, editing and publishing — Poetry and prose in Bahasa Melayu and English since 1995, five books to date, and, since 2025, Langgam Fikir Enterprise, an imprint in Seri Kembangan, Selangor that publishes my own titles in literature, history and religious discourse.
  • Tools for the open web — WordPress plugins and web utilities that solve unglamorous problems well: clean citations, structured data, readable long-form posts, typography that behaves. All of it public on GitHub.
